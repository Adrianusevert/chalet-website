The chalet is located within walking distance of the village centre in Sankt Lambrecht, a quiet mountain village in the district of Murau. Inside you will find an open living space with exposed timber beams, a dining table for the whole party and large windows with mountain views.

The Grebenzen slopes run right past the house and the gondola base station is less than 100 metres away: clip on your skis at the front door. The bedrooms and the sauna are on the ground floor, with the living floor, kitchen and balcony above. Parking is available on the property, with an EV charging point.

Zirbitzkogel-Grebenzen

Sankt Lambrecht and surroundings

Sankt Lambrecht is best known for its Benedictine abbey and its location in the Zirbitzkogel-Grebenzen nature park. The village has shops and restaurants for daily needs and a good meal.

Summer

  • Hiking in the Zirbitzkogel-Grebenzen nature park, from valley walks to the summit of the Zirbitzkogel (2,396 m), including the 130-kilometre Via Natura themed trail
  • Golf at the 18-hole Grebenzen-Mariahof course (par 72, six water hazards), around 7 km away
  • Waterfalls: the Gragger gorge with its cascade waterfall near Neumarkt, and the Günster waterfall, at 65 metres the highest in Styria
  • Cycling, e-biking and mountain biking, including the Mur cycle path (Murradweg)
  • Visiting the Benedictine abbey of St. Lambrecht, with guided tours in summer
  • Swimming and fishing at the nature park's lakes and ponds, such as Furtner Teich and Auerlingsee
  • A ride on the nostalgic steam train along the Murtalbahn from Murau

Winter

  • Ski-in ski-out: the Grebenzen slopes run past the house, with the gondola less than 100 metres away
  • Larger ski resorts such as Kreischberg near Murau within driving distance
  • More than 20 kilometres of cross-country trails, plus winter hiking routes, snowshoe tours and tobogganing in the nature park
  • Quiet slopes and short queues, ideal for families

Grebenzen ski area: ski-in ski-out

The house sits right on the slope. The Grebenzen runs pass right by the house and the gondola base station is less than 100 metres away. Clip on your skis at the front door in the morning, and step straight into the sauna in the afternoon.

  • 27 km of pistes with the Styrian slope quality seal, quiet and wide
  • Modern 10-person gondola, chairlift and seven drag lifts
  • Children's area with baby lift and magic carpet, ski school and rental at the base station
  • Snow-sure: snowmaking between 1,000 and 1,900 metres above sea level
  • Ski depot and free wifi at the base station
  • Natural toboggan run and marked ski touring routes
  • Discounts up to age 28; part of the Mur-Mürz Top Ski Pass and Styria Joker
